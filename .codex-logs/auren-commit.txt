provider response, while account management still mixed practice-only

removal and preventing automatic re-addition with tombstones. Validate
ownership and metadata, preserve settings offline, and reject malformed
provider lists instead of treating them as authoritative empty results.

tabs, platform branding and a query-preserving legacy redirect. Show Lucid
plan references and confirmed DLL, consistency, target and MLL settings
without inventing live risk headroom from monthly performance. Preserve

selector in mobile Settings. Keep armed Buy, Sell and Auto states visible,
provide Cancel, delegate sheet requests to one persistent controller and
cancel Auto when disconnected or switching to market-only controls. Keep

browser-managed soft stops throughout previews and position presentation.

checks. Recover streams with bounded fast retry, retire lost-pong sockets,
